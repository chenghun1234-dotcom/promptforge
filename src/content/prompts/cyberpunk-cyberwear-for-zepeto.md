### Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto

The fusion of cyberpunk aesthetics with the digital world of Zepeto offers a vast playground for creators to craft unique, futuristic outfits that embody the essence of avant-garde fashion and cutting-edge technology. This guide is designed to help professional creators leverage AI to generate stunning cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto, with a focus on detail, originality, and marketability.

### Understanding the Prompt

The main prompt is crafted to inspire the generation of highly detailed and futuristic cyberpunk outfits. Key elements include:

- **Neon-lit accents** to give the outfit a vibrant, high-tech appearance.
- **Metallic textures** to add depth and a sense of advanced materials.
- **Intricate circuitry patterns** to embody the cyberpunk theme of integrating technology into fashion.

The negative prompt is essential for guiding the AI away from common pitfalls such as:

- **Overly simplistic designs** that lack the complexity and detail expected of cyberpunk fashion.
- **Lack of texture** which can make the outfit appear flat and uninteresting.
- **Uninspired color palettes** that do not align with the vibrant, futuristic vibe of cyberpunk.
- **Real-world logos, trademarks, or copyrighted materials** to avoid legal issues and ensure the originality of the design.

### Technical Settings for Optimal Results

To achieve the desired level of detail and realism in your cyberpunk cyberwear designs, the following technical settings are recommended:

- **Sampler:** DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model) for its ability to produce high-quality images with detailed textures and patterns.
- **CFG:** 7.5 to balance between the adherence to the prompt and the generation of novel, interesting features.
- **Steps:** 50 to allow for a thorough exploration of the design space and refinement of the generated outfit.
