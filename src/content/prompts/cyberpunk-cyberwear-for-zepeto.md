As a professional creator selling digital assets, staying ahead of the curve in terms of trends and demand is crucial. Cyberpunk themes have seen a resurgence in popularity, especially with the rise of virtual worlds and avatars like Zepeto. This guide is designed to help you create stunning cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto using AI, focusing on professional-grade outputs that can be monetized effectively.

## Understanding the Prompt
The key to generating high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto lies in crafting a detailed and specific prompt. The main prompt should evoke a futuristic, high-tech feel, incorporating elements like neon lights, metallic textures, and dynamic poses. Conversely, the negative prompt helps refine the output by excluding unwanted features such as low-poly models, cartoonish styles, and natural environments.

### Main Prompt
`Cyberpunk-inspired futuristic outfit for Zepeto avatar, neon-lit cityscape background, high-tech gadgets, metallic and leather textures, vibrant colors, intricate details, dynamic pose.`

### Negative Prompt
`low-poly, cartoonish, simplistic, bright colors, daytime, natural environment, basic textures`

## Technical Settings
For optimal results, consider the following technical settings:

- **Sampler:** DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model) is recommended for its ability to produce detailed and coherent images.
- **CFG (Classifier Free Guidance):** A CFG of 7.5 offers a good balance between adhering to the prompt and allowing for creative freedom.
- **Steps:** 50 steps should provide sufficient iterations for the model to refine the image without overfitting.
