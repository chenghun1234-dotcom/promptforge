Cyberpunk is a captivating genre that has inspired countless creators and fans alike with its dystopian futures, advanced technologies, and striking aesthetics. ## Understanding the Prompt
To generate high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear, it's essential to craft a precise prompt that guides the AI model towards your desired outcome. The main prompt should evoke the essence of cyberpunk, emphasizing futuristic elements, neon accents, and avant-garde designs. Meanwhile, the negative prompt helps refine the output by excluding unwanted characteristics such as overly bright colors or simplistic patterns.

### Pro Prompt
- **Main Prompt:** Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde designs.
- **Negative Prompt:** Avoid generating outfits with bright colors, cartoonish designs, or overly simplistic patterns.
