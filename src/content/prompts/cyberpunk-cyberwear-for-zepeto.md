### Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear
Cyberpunk fashion is all about embracing a futuristic, high-tech aesthetic with a blend of neon lights, metallic textures, and avant-garde accessories. When creating cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto, focus on sleek, high-tech design elements and bold, vibrant colors to capture the essence of this genre.

### Pro Prompt
The main prompt is designed to guide the AI in generating highly detailed, futuristic cyberpunk-themed outfits. The negative prompt helps avoid undesirable elements in the generated designs.

- **Main Prompt:** Generate a highly detailed, futuristic cyberpunk-themed outfit for Zepeto, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde accessories, with a focus on sleek, high-tech design elements and bold, vibrant colors.
- **Negative Prompt:** Avoid creating outfits with overly simplistic designs, muted colors, or lack of futuristic details, and do not include any real-world logos, trademarks, or copyrighted materials.
