### Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto

Cyberpunk, a genre that has captivated audiences with its futuristic, high-tech worldview, offers a rich tapestry of inspiration for digital fashion. Creating cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto, a popular metaverse platform, presents an exciting opportunity for pro creators to showcase their talent and monetize their digital assets. This guide is designed to help you navigate the process of generating stunning cyberpunk cyberwear using AI, with a focus on technical settings and monetization strategies.

### Crafting the Perfect Prompt

The key to successful AI-generated content lies in the prompt. For cyberpunk cyberwear, your main prompt should evoke the essence of futuristic, avant-garde fashion, emphasizing neon lights, metallic textures, and high-tech accessories. 

**Main Prompt:** `Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for Zepeto, including neon-lit jackets, metallic pants, and high-tech accessories, with intricate details and a focus on avant-garde fashion.`

To refine your results, a negative prompt is crucial. It helps the AI avoid common pitfalls and undesirable elements in your designs.

**Negative Prompt:** `Avoid generating outfits with overly simplistic designs, lack of neon or metallic elements, and accessories that do not resemble cyberpunk themes.`

### Technical Settings for Optimal Results

Achieving the desired level of detail and authenticity in your cyberpunk cyberwear requires careful adjustment of technical settings.

- **Sampler:** Euler a is recommended for its ability to produce detailed, high-quality images with a good balance between speed and quality.
- **CFG (Classifier Free Guidance):** A CFG of 7.5 offers a strong guidance towards the prompt without overfitting, allowing for creative freedom while maintaining the cyberpunk theme.
- **Steps:** 50 steps provide ample iterations for the AI to refine the design, ensuring intricate details and a polished look.
