### Introduction
The demand for unique, high-quality digital assets is on the rise, particularly in the realm of cyberpunk-inspired fashion. By leveraging AI technology, creators can produce intricate, futuristic designs that captivate audiences and set their digital stores apart from the competition.

### Pro Prompt
The key to generating exceptional cyberpunk cyberwear lies in the prompt. The main prompt should evoke a sense of futurism and high-tech elegance, while the negative prompt ensures that the output avoids common pitfalls such as oversimplification or copyright infringement.

- **Main Prompt:** `Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and sleek, high-tech accessories, with a focus on intricate details and realistic materials.`
- **Negative Prompt:** `Avoid generating outfits with overly simplistic designs, cartoonish elements, or low-poly textures, and exclude any assets that resemble real-world copyrighted materials.`

### Technical Settings
Achieving the perfect balance of detail and realism requires careful adjustment of the technical settings.

- **Sampler:** `DDIM` offers a good balance between speed and quality, making it ideal for most cyberwear generation tasks.
- **CFG:** A setting of `7.5` provides a high level of detail without overfitting, allowing for the creation of complex, futuristic designs.
- **Steps:** `50` steps are typically sufficient to generate high-quality assets with well-defined features and textures.
