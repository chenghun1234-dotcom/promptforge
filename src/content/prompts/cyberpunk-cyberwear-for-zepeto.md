### Crafting the Perfect Prompt

To create compelling cyberpunk cyberwear, your AI prompt needs to be both detailed and focused. The main prompt should evoke the essence of cyberpunk fashion, emphasizing neon accents, metallic textures, and sleek accessories. Meanwhile, the negative prompt ensures that the AI avoids generic or uninspired designs, pushing the boundaries of avant-garde fashion.

- **Main Prompt:** "Create a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and sleek, high-tech accessories, with a focus on avant-garde fashion and cutting-edge style."
- **Negative Prompt:** "Avoid creating outfits with overly simplistic designs, lackluster colors, or uninspired accessories, and refrain from incorporating elements that are not distinctly cyberpunk in nature."
