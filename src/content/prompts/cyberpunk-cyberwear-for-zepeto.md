The key to successful AI generation is a well-crafted prompt. For cyberpunk cyberwear, consider elements such as:
- **Neon Accents:** Neon lights, neon paint, or neon-infused materials can add a striking, futuristic glow to your designs.
- **Metallic Textures:** Incorporating metallic textures can give your cyberwear a sleek, high-tech feel, perfect for a cyberpunk theme.
- **Avant-Garde Design:** Cyberpunk fashion often pushes boundaries, so don't be afraid to think outside the box with unconventional shapes, asymmetry, and innovative material uses.
