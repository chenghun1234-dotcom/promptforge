## Technical Settings for Optimal Results

To achieve the best results, it's crucial to adjust the technical settings of your AI model. The recommended settings are as follows:

- **Sampler:** DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model) is chosen for its ability to produce high-quality images with detailed textures and patterns, which are essential for capturing the intricate designs often found in cyberpunk fashion.
- **CFG (Classifier-Free Guidance):** A CFG scale of 7.5 strikes a balance between adhering to the prompt and allowing for creative freedom. This setting encourages the model to generate unique and interesting designs while staying true to the cyberpunk aesthetic.
- **Steps:** 50 steps provide a sufficient number of iterations for the model to refine its output, ensuring that the generated images are of high quality and detail.
