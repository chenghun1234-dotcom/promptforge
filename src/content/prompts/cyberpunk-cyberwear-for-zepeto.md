To create stunning cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto, follow these professional AI prompt guidelines:

### Introduction
Cyberpunk fashion is all about embracing a futuristic, high-tech aesthetic, and Zepeto's digital platform is the perfect place to showcase your designs. With the right AI prompts, you can create unique, eye-catching cyberwear that stands out from the crowd.

### Pro Prompt
Use the following main prompt and negative prompt to guide your AI generation:
* **Main Prompt:** Create a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for Zepeto, including a sleek black jumpsuit with neon green accents, a pair of mirrored shades, and a metallic exosuit accessory. The outfit should have a high-tech, avant-garde feel, with intricate details and a sense of dynamic movement.
* **Negative Prompt:** Avoid creating an outfit that looks too casual, simplistic, or lacking in detail. Do not include any bright or pastel colors, and avoid using overly bulky or cumbersome designs. The outfit should not resemble any existing or copyrighted characters.
