## Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto
Cyberpunk fashion has become a staple in the world of Zepeto, with users eager to express themselves through futuristic and high-tech outfits. As a professional creator, you can capitalize on this trend by generating unique and stunning cyberpunk cyberwear using AI.

## Crafting the Perfect Prompt
To create high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear, it's essential to craft a well-structured prompt that guides the AI model towards your desired outcome. The main prompt should include key elements such as:
* High-fashion cyberpunk outfit
* Neon-lit accents
* Metallic textures
* Futuristic accessories

The negative prompt should exclude unwanted features, such as:
* Overly simplistic designs
* Lack of neon accents
* Non-futuristic elements

## Technical Settings for AI Generation
To achieve the best results, use the following technical settings:
* **Sampler:** Euler a
* **CFG:** 7.5
* **Steps:** 50

These settings will allow the AI model to generate highly detailed and realistic cyberpunk cyberwear designs.
