To generate exceptional cyberpunk cyberwear, your AI prompt needs to be precise and evocative. Consider the following:

- **Main Prompt:** `Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde accessories, with a focus on high-fashion appeal and intricate details.`
