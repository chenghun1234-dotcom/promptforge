## Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto

The cyberpunk genre has always been a fascination for many, with its blend of high-tech innovation and gritty, dystopian aesthetics. For creators looking to capitalize on this trend in the digital realm, especially within platforms like Zepeto, crafting unique and captivating cyberpunk-inspired cyberwear is a promising venture. This guide is designed to help professional creators navigate the process of generating and monetizing such assets using AI tools.

## Crafting the Perfect AI Prompt

The key to successful AI-generated content lies in the prompt. A well-crafted prompt should be detailed, specifying the desired outcome while leaving room for the AI's creative interpretation.

- **Main Prompt**: "Generate a highly detailed, futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto avatar, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and sleek, high-tech accessories, with a focus on vibrant colors and dynamic lighting effects."
- **Negative Prompt**: "Avoid generating outfits with realistic or historical elements, and do not include any logos, trademarks, or copyrighted materials, focusing solely on original, futuristic designs."

## Technical Settings for Optimal Results

To achieve the best results, it's crucial to adjust the technical settings of your AI generation tool. The following settings are recommended for this specific task:

- **Sampler**: DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model) for its ability to produce high-quality, detailed images with a good balance between speed and quality.
- **CFG (Classifier-Free Guidance)**: 7.5, which offers a strong guidance towards the prompt without overly constraining the creativity of the model.
- **Steps**: 50, allowing for a comprehensive and detailed generation process that captures the intricacies of cyberpunk design.

Additionally, offer custom commission services where clients can request unique, personalized designs based on their preferences and specifications.
