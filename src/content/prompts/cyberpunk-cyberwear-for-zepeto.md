## Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto
To create stunning cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto, you'll need to balance futuristic elements with wearable, avant-garde fashion. This guide is designed for professional creators looking to generate and sell unique digital assets.

## Crafting the Perfect Prompt
Your main prompt should focus on the core elements of cyberpunk fashion, including neon accents, metallic textures, and futuristic patterns. The negative prompt will help refine your results by excluding simplistic designs and ensuring your outfit fits within the cyberpunk aesthetic.

### Pro Prompt Example
- **Main Prompt:** Generate a highly detailed cyberpunk-themed outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and futuristic patterns, with a focus on wearable technology and avant-garde fashion.
- **Negative Prompt:** Avoid generating outfits with overly simplistic designs, lack of texture, or those that do not fit within the cyberpunk aesthetic, and ensure the outfit is not too similar to existing Zepeto assets.

## Technical Settings for Optimal Results
For the best results, consider the following technical settings:
- **Sampler:** DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model) for more controlled and detailed outputs.
- **CFG:** 7.5 to balance detail and creativity.
- **Steps:** 50 for a thorough generation process that captures all desired elements.

## Monetizing Your Cyberpunk Cyberwear
To monetize your cyberpunk cyberwear, consider selling your assets on the Zepeto marketplace or offering custom commissions. Highlight the exclusivity and rarity of your AI-generated designs to attract buyers looking for unique outfits.

### Monetization Tip
Sell your generated cyberpunk cyberwear as exclusive digital assets on platforms like the Zepeto marketplace, or offer custom commissions for unique outfits tailored to individual buyers' preferences, highlighting the rarity and exclusivity of AI-generated designs.
