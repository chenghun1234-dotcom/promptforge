To create stunning cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto using AI, follow this professional guide:

### Introduction
The cyberpunk genre has gained immense popularity in recent years, and Zepeto characters are the perfect canvas for showcasing futuristic fashion. As a professional creator, you can capitalize on this trend by designing and selling unique cyberpunk-inspired digital assets.

### Pro Prompt
The key to generating high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear is to provide a detailed and specific prompt. Use the following main prompt and negative prompt to guide the AI:

* **Main Prompt:** Cyberpunk-inspired futuristic clothing and accessories for a Zepeto character, with neon lights, metallic textures, and sleek designs, including jackets, pants, hats, and glasses, in a high-tech cityscape background.
* **Negative Prompt:** No cartoonish, childish, or overly simplistic designs, no bright or pastel colors, no fantasy or magical elements, no historical or traditional clothing.

### Technical Settings
To achieve the desired outcome, adjust the technical settings as follows:

* **Sampler:** DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model)
* **CFG:** 7.5 (This controls the level of detail and realism in the generated image)
* **Steps:** 50 (This determines the number of iterations the AI will perform to refine the image)
