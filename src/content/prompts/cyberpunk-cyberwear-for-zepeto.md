### Crafting the Perfect Prompt
To generate high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear, your AI prompt should be detailed and specific. The main prompt should evoke the essence of cyberpunk, including elements like neon lights, metallic surfaces, and futuristic accessories. Conversely, the negative prompt ensures that the generated content stays within the desired theme by excluding unwanted elements.

- **Main Prompt:** `Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde accessories, with a focus on high-fashion and cutting-edge technology.`
- **Negative Prompt:** `Avoid generating outfits with realistic or historical elements, and do not include any logos or branding from existing companies.`
