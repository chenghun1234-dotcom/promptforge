The cyberpunk genre has seen a resurgence in popularity, particularly in the realm of digital fashion. This guide is tailored for professional creators looking to capitalize on this trend by producing high-quality, unique digital assets.

### Crafting the Perfect Prompt

To create stunning cyberpunk cyberwear, your AI prompt needs to be precise and detailed. The **main prompt** should guide the AI towards generating futuristic, high-fashion designs with neon accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde silhouettes. The **negative prompt** is crucial for avoiding clichés and ensuring the designs are original and sophisticated.

### Technical Settings for Optimal Results

- **Sampler:** DPM++ offers a good balance between detail and speed for generating complex cyberwear designs.
- **CFG:** A setting of 7.5 provides a strong guidance towards the prompt while allowing for creative freedom.
- **Steps:** 50 steps are sufficient for most designs, but you may need to adjust based on the complexity of your prompt.
