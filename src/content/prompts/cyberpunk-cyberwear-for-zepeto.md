## Crafting the Perfect Prompt
To generate high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear, it's essential to craft a well-structured prompt that guides the AI model. Our recommended prompt is:
"Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde accessories."
This prompt provides a clear direction for the AI model, ensuring that the generated outfit is both futuristic and cyberpunk-inspired.

## Negative Prompting for Better Results
Negative prompting is a technique used to exclude unwanted elements from the generated output. For this project, we recommend using the following negative prompt:
"Avoid generating outfits with realistic or historical elements, and do not include any logos or branding."
This negative prompt helps the AI model avoid generating outfits that are too realistic or include unwanted branding, resulting in a more unique and cyberpunk-inspired design.

* **Sampler:** Euler a
* **CFG:** 7.5
* **Steps:** 50
These settings provide a good balance between quality and generation time, allowing you to produce high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear quickly and efficiently.
