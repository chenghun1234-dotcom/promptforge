## Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto
As a professional creator of digital assets, staying ahead of the curve in terms of style and demand is crucial. The cyberpunk genre, with its futuristic, high-tech, and often dystopian themes, offers a rich canvas for creativity, especially when it comes to designing cyberwear for platforms like Zepeto. This guide is designed to help you navigate the process of creating stunning cyberpunk-inspired digital assets using AI prompts, tailored specifically for the Zepeto audience.

## Crafting the Perfect Prompt
The key to generating high-quality digital assets with AI lies in the prompt. A well-crafted prompt should be detailed, specific, and evoke the exact style and theme you're aiming for.

- **Main Prompt:** "Cyberpunk-inspired futuristic outfit for Zepeto, neon-lit, metallic accents, and sleek design, high-fashion, detailed textures, and accessories." This prompt sets the foundation for your cyberpunk theme, emphasizing the need for a futuristic, high-fashion design that incorporates neon lights and metallic accents, perfect for the Zepeto platform.
- **Negative Prompt:** "low-poly, simplistic, cartoonish, or outdated designs, avoid bright colors, and focus on muted tones with neon highlights." The negative prompt is just as important, as it helps the AI algorithm understand what to avoid. By excluding low-poly, simplistic, and outdated designs, you ensure your generated asset is detailed and modern.

## Technical Settings for Optimal Results
The technical settings of your AI generation tool can significantly impact the quality and style of your output. Here are some recommended settings for generating cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto:

- **Sampler:** Euler a - This sampler is known for its ability to produce consistent and high-quality results, especially with complex prompts.
- **CFG:** 7.5 - The CFG (Classifier Free Guidance) scale controls how closely the generation follows the prompt. A higher value like 7.5 ensures that the output closely matches your cyberpunk theme without being too rigid.
- **Steps:** 50 - The number of steps affects the detail and refinement of the generated image. 50 steps provide a good balance between detail and processing time for most cyberpunk cyberwear designs.
