## Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto
As a professional creator, you can capitalize on the growing demand for unique digital assets in the Zepeto universe by generating stunning cyberpunk-inspired outfits. This guide will walk you through the process of creating futuristic cyberwear using AI, and provide tips on how to monetize your designs.

## Crafting the Perfect Prompt
To generate high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear, you need a well-crafted prompt that balances creativity with technical specificity. The main prompt should focus on the key elements of cyberpunk fashion, including neon accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde silhouettes. The negative prompt should exclude any unwanted elements, such as realistic or historical details, and ensure that the generated design is entirely original.

## Technical Settings for Optimal Results
To achieve the best possible results, it's essential to fine-tune your technical settings. We recommend using the Euler a sampler, which provides a good balance between detail and diversity. A CFG (Classifier-Free Guidance) scale of 7.5 will help to maintain a consistent level of quality, while 50 steps will allow for sufficient iteration and refinement.

## Monetizing Your Digital Assets
Once you've generated your cyberpunk cyberwear designs, you can sell them as exclusive digital assets on online marketplaces like the Zepeto Store. Alternatively, you can offer custom commissions to clients looking for unique, one-of-a-kind outfits for their Zepeto characters. By leveraging your creativity and technical skills, you can tap into the lucrative market for digital fashion and build a successful business as a professional creator.

## Example Use Case
To get started, try using the following prompt:
"Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde silhouettes, with a focus on sleek, high-tech details and bold, vibrant colors.
