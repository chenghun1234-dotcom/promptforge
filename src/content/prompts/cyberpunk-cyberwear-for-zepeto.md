To create stunning cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto using AI, follow this professional prompt guide:

### Introduction
The cyberpunk genre has gained immense popularity in recent years, and Zepeto characters are the perfect canvas to showcase this aesthetic. With the right AI prompt, you can generate unique and captivating cyberwear designs that will stand out in the digital world.

### Pro Prompt
The key to generating high-quality cyberpunk cyberwear lies in the prompt. Use the following main prompt and negative prompt to guide the AI:

* **Main Prompt:** Generate a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and sleek, high-tech accessories, with a focus on intricate details and vibrant colors.
* **Negative Prompt:** Avoid generating outfits with overly simplistic designs, muted colors, or lack of futuristic elements, and do not include any copyrighted or trademarked symbols, logos, or characters.

### Technical Settings
To achieve the desired results, use the following technical settings:

* **Sampler:** DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model)
* **CFG:** 7.5 (This controls the level of detail and realism in the generated image)
* **Steps:** 50 (This determines the number of iterations the AI will perform to refine the image)
