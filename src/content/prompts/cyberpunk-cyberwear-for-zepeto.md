## Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto
As a professional creator, you're likely familiar with the growing demand for unique digital assets in virtual worlds like Zepeto. Cyberpunk-themed cyberwear is particularly popular, offering a blend of futuristic aesthetics and high-fashion sensibilities. This guide will walk you through creating stunning cyberpunk cyberwear for Zepeto using AI, and provide tips on how to monetize your digital assets.

## Crafting the Perfect Prompt
To create exceptional cyberpunk cyberwear, you need a well-crafted prompt that guides the AI in generating your desired outcome. The main prompt should focus on the key elements of cyberpunk fashion, including neon accents, metallic textures, and sleek lines. The negative prompt is crucial for avoiding unwanted features in your design, such as overly simplistic elements or bright primary colors.

### Pro Prompt
- **Main Prompt:** Create a futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for a Zepeto character, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and sleek lines, with a focus on high-fashion and avant-garde design elements.
- **Negative Prompt:** Avoid creating outfits with overly simplistic designs, bright primary colors, or lack of futuristic elements, and do not include any logos or branding from existing companies.
