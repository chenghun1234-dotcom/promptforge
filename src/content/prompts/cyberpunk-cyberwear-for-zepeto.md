## Understanding the Prompt
The key to successful AI generation is crafting a well-defined prompt. For cyberpunk cyberwear, consider the following elements:
- **Futuristic and High-Tech**: Incorporate neon lights, metallic textures, and futuristic patterns.
- **Sleek and Modern**: Aim for designs that are avant-garde and push the boundaries of fashion.
- **Character Compatibility**: Ensure the outfit is designed with Zepeto characters in mind, considering their body types and customization options.

## Technical Settings for Generation
To achieve high-quality results, adjust your AI generation settings as follows:
- **Sampler**: Euler a provides a good balance between detail and speed.
- **CFG (Classifier Free Guidance)**: A setting of 7.5 helps in generating more diverse and detailed outcomes.
- **Steps**: 50 steps are sufficient for most designs, but you can adjust based on the complexity of your prompt.
