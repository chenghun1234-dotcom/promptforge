### Technical Settings
- **Sampler:** DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Model) is recommended for its ability to produce highly detailed and realistic images with less noise compared to other samplers.

- **Steps:** 50 steps are sufficient for most prompts, allowing the model to refine the image adequately without over-processing it, which can lead to undesirable artifacts.
