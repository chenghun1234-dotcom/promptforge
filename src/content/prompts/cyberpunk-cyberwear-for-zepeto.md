## Introduction to Cyberpunk Cyberwear for Zepeto
Cyberpunk, a genre that explores the intersection of technology and humanity, offers a rich backdrop for creating futuristic and avant-garde digital fashion. For creators looking to tap into the vibrant world of Zepeto, designing cyberpunk cyberwear can be a lucrative venture, given the platform's emphasis on personal expression and customization.

## Crafting the Perfect Prompt
To generate high-quality, cyberpunk-inspired cyberwear for Zepeto, it's essential to craft a detailed and specific prompt. The main prompt should guide the AI towards creating futuristic, neon-lit, and metallic outfits that embody the essence of cyberpunk. Meanwhile, the negative prompt helps avoid common pitfalls such as lack of originality or failure to incorporate key cyberpunk elements.

### Example Prompt
- **Main Prompt:** Generate a highly detailed, futuristic cyberpunk-inspired outfit for Zepeto, including neon-lit accents, metallic textures, and avant-garde design elements, with a focus on wearable technology and innovative materials.
- **Negative Prompt:** Avoid generating outfits that are too similar to existing designs, lack originality, or do not incorporate cyberpunk elements; exclude overly simplistic or childish designs.

## Technical Settings for Optimal Generation
Achieving the desired level of detail and realism in your cyberpunk cyberwear requires adjusting the technical settings of your AI generation tool. 

- **Sampler:** Euler a offers a good balance between detail and speed for most applications.
- **CFG (Classifier Free Guidance):** A CFG of 7.5 provides a strong guidance towards the prompt without overfitting.
- **Steps:** 50 steps are typically sufficient for generating complex designs with ample detail.
